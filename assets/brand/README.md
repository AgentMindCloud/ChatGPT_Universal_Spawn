# Brand use

The portal mark represents small tools entering a conversation-driven workflow and emerging as one usable capability. Use `logo-light.svg` on light backgrounds, `logo-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds, and `logo-mark.svg` when space is limited.

- Keep clear space equal to one tool-node diameter around the mark.
- Keep the mark at least 32 px wide.
- Use the supplied colors or a single high-contrast color.
- Do not rotate, stretch, add gradients, or rearrange the nodes.
- Do not combine the mark with the OpenAI or ChatGPT logo.
- Do not describe this independent AgentMindCloud project as endorsed, certified, or published by OpenAI.
